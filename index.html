Welcome to France

France is a country in Western Europe known for its beautiful cities, rich history, art, fashion and delicious food. Its capital city is Paris. France attracts millions of tourists who visit its famous landmarks, museums, beaches and castles.

Five Tourist Attractions in France

1. Eiffel Tower

The Eiffel Tower is one of the most famous landmarks in the world. It is located in Paris and offers beautiful views of the city.

2. Louvre Museum

The Louvre is a famous art museum in Paris. It houses thousands of artworks, including the well-known Mona Lisa painting.

3. Notre-Dame Cathedral

Notre-Dame is a historic cathedral in Paris known for its Gothic architecture and impressive design. It is an important French cultural landmark.

4. French Riviera

The French Riviera is a beautiful Mediterranean coastal region known for its beaches, sunny weather and attractive seaside towns.

5. Palace of Versailles

The Palace of Versailles is a grand historic palace near Paris. It is famous for its royal history, magnificent rooms and beautiful gardens.
